Good to know

Frequently Asked

Everything people usually ask before their first visit.

Do you take walk-ins, or is it reservation only?

Walk-ins are welcome, but tables fill fast on weekends. We'd recommend reserving ahead for Friday and Saturday nights to avoid a wait.

Do you have vegetarian or vegan options?

Yes — several vegetarian dishes are marked on the menu. For vegan requests, let our staff know when you order and the kitchen can adjust most pasta and pizza dishes.

Is parking available?

There's free street parking along Fornace Lane, and a paid parking lot two minutes' walk from the entrance.

Can you accommodate large groups?

For groups above 8, we recommend calling ahead so we can prepare a table setup in advance. For anything above 12, check our Events page for private dining options.

Is there a dress code?

Smart casual is fine. No strict dress code, but we'd suggest avoiding beachwear for the evening seating.

Do you offer delivery or takeaway?

Currently dine-in only. We're a small kitchen and prefer every dish leaves the oven straight to your table.

Can I book the entire restaurant for a private event?

Yes — full buyouts are available for weddings, engagements, and large corporate events. See the Events & Private Dining page for packages and pricing.
