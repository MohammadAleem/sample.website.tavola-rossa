About Tavola Rossa

One oven, one family, no shortcuts — since 1987.

The kitchen

Tavola Rossa started as a single wood oven in a rented kitchen and a family recipe book held together with tape. Three decades later, the oven is bigger, the book is the same. We still roll the pasta every morning, still char the crust until it blisters, and still close the doors when the flour runs out — not a minute later.

Our promise

Every dish leaving this kitchen is made the same day it's served. No frozen dough, no bottled sauce, no pasta from a packet. If we run out of something fresh, it comes off the menu for the night — it doesn't get replaced with a shortcut.
